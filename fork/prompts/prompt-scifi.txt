Write a science-fiction short story of about 900 words. The crew of a generation ship wakes from cryosleep above the planet they were sent to settle and finds its cities already lit: the colonists below are their own descendants, from a copy of the ship that left a century after them and arrived first. Tell it through the eyes of the ship's archivist. Literary prose with sensory detail and dialogue; no headings, no lists, no code.
