### Part 3: Explanation of Integration by Parts

**General Concept:**
Integration by parts is derived from the product rule for differentiation. It allows us to integrate a product of two functions by transferring the derivative from one function to the other. It is most useful when the integrand is a product of two different types of functions (e.g., polynomial $\times$ exponential, logarithmic $\times$ trigonometric).

**The Strategy (LIATE Rule):**
When choosing which part of the product to set as $u$ (to be differentiated) and which to set as $dv$ (to be integrated), a helpful mnemonic is **LIATE**:
