The ocean breathes in black,
swallowing the horizon’s edge.

its glass eye unblinking.
Far out, a skiff rides the swell,
hull thin as a whisper.

Stars fracture on the water’s skin,
scattered like crushed glass.

but the boat turns toward it.
Distance stretches, rope-taut,
shore lost in the murk.
Night holds its breath,
waiting for the dawn’s first pale line.
The sea remembers nothing,
only the rhythm of the tide.
Light and dark meet in the wake,
where the small boat disappears.
