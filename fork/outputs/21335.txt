The black water swells, a restless beast
breathing in the dark,
its surface rippling with the ghostly
reflections of a moon that refuses
to stay still.

Far out, the lighthouse stands
like a solitary finger
pointing into the void,
casting a cone of amber light
that cuts through the heavy mist.

It is a beacon for the lost,
a steady pulse against the chaos,
guiding the eye through the
endless expanse of silence.

A small boat drifts in the shadows,
its hull barely visible,
a speck of wood and canvas
floating on the edge of the abyss.

The engine hums a low, rhythmic

rusted iron of the pier.

knowing that safety is a
distance measured in miles,
not in comfort.

it only moves,
swallowing the light,
swallowing the boat,
swallowing the night.
