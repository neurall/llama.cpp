The black water swells, a restless beast
breathing in the dark,
its surface rippling with the ghostly
reflections of a moon that refuses
to stay still.

Far out, the lighthouse stands
like a solitary finger
pointing into the void,
casting a beam of pale gold
that cuts through the heavy mist.

It is a beacon for the lost,
a steady pulse against the chaos,
guiding the eye through the
endless expanse of silence.

A small boat drifts in the shadows,
its hull barely visible,
rocking gently with the tide.
The captain holds the wheel tight,
eyes fixed on the distant light,
knowing that shore is a dream
now, and only the sea remains.

The waves whisper secrets
to the wood and the steel,
stories of storms past
and the cold, deep hunger
of the ocean floor.

Here, the world shrinks
to the size of a cabin,
to the sound of the engine
humming low and steady,
a heartbeat in the night.

The light flickers, then holds,
a promise of safety
in the vast, unyielding dark.
